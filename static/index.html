How to play
  • 2–6 traders share one live market for 5 minutes. Richest at the closing bell wins.
  • Every buy pushes the price up, every sell pushes it down — for everyone.
  • News hits every 20–40s; prices react ~2s after the headline. Unconfirmed rumors may be true… or planted.
  • Short to profit from falling prices. Take loans for leverage — repay them before the bell.
  • Hold >50% of all player-held units of a commodity to corner it and set a price floor.
  • Play your secret cards; earn more at profit milestones. Fulfil contracts for big rewards.
  • Last 30 seconds: closing frenzy — volatility doubles!
